# Ambiguities and decisions: TypeScript engine (`src/engine/`)

Role: ts-engine. Every entry says what was unclear, the reading implemented, and why. The engine was
written from `docs/spec/00`–`05` only (no backend code, no oracle). The golden vectors were run only
after the engine and its own tests passed.

**How to read the "pinned" column.** After the vectors existed I re-ran them against an
_alternative_ reading of each item (a throw-away patch, since reverted). `pinned` means the
alternative made at least one vector fail, so the vectors confirm the reading. `not pinned` means
the vectors pass under both readings; if the Python engine chose differently, nothing in the
current vectors will notice, so those entries are where the two engines can silently diverge.
Vectors seen: 44 `analysis-*.json` + 173 normalization cases.

## A. `analyze()`

| E1  | 01 §4, 02 §2–3       | "string compare of the joined key" / "source role key string" never names a separator.                 | Parts joined with `\|`: `kind\|ref\|id\|<posting_id>` and `kind\|ref\|k\|<company_key>\|<title_key>\|<location_key>`, compared by code point. Matters when two role keys of one source differ at a point where `\|` (0x7C) outranks a space or `-`, e.g. removal-event order for `acme\|…` vs `acme robotics\|…`. | E2  | 02 §2 last para., §5 | Does a company exist because a _posting_ names it, or because a _source role_ currently belongs to it? | Companies exist only for source roles, assigned by the role's **latest** `company_name`. A company therefore always has at least one role, and a role renamed to another company moves entirely (history, events) to the new one.                                                                                                                         | not pinned                                                  |
| E3  | 01 §3.2, 02 §5       | "earliest observed posting", "raw company names of the company's postings".                            | Postings are those of the company's source roles, across all processed snapshots, **including** postings superseded by a later duplicate in the same snapshot and names the role carried before a rename. So a renamed role leaves its old name in `aliases`, and may supply `name` if it was observed earliest.                                          | not pinned (superseded duplicates), not exercised (renames) |
| E4  | 01 §3.3–3.4          | Decision keys that match no company (e.g. `merge(a, x)`, `merge(x, b)`, `x` absent).                   | They take part in the union-find, so merges are transitive through them, and the group's canonical key is its smallest member key even when that key has no postings (`key`, `company_id` follow it).                                                                                                                                                     | not pinned                                                  |
| E5  | 01 §3.3–3.4          | A `not_same(a, b)` whose keys are no longer the canonical keys of their groups after merges.           | The candidate pair of groups is suppressed when _any_ member of one group is declared `not_same` against _any_ member of the other. ## B. CSV (`csv.ts`)

| #   | Spec | Unclear                                                                 | Reading implemented                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| C1  | 05   | "≤1 MB, ≤5,000 data rows".                                              | 1 MiB = 1,048,576 **UTF-8 bytes**. Row cap counts non-blank records after the header. Exceeding either rejects the whole file (file-level error, no truncation).                                                                                                                                                                           |
| C2  | 05   | "1-based CSV line number".                                              | Physical line on which the record **starts**; newlines inside quoted fields are counted. The header is line 1 unless blank lines precede it.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| C3  | 05   | Header normalization.                                                   | Trim, lowercase, every whitespace character or hyphen → `_`; no run-collapsing. Duplicates are detected after normalization. A blank header cell is ignored with a warning (not a duplicate error).                                                                                                                                        |
| C4  | 05   | Shape errors not in the list.                                           | A row with extra non-empty cells is rejected (shifted columns would corrupt data); trailing empty cells and short rows are tolerated (padded). Text after a closing quote rejects the row; a quote inside an unquoted field is literal; a lone CR ends a record.                                                                           |
| C5  | 05   | `observed_at` is per row but an import is one snapshot.                 | Rows are grouped into one `additive` snapshot per distinct effective `observed_at` (ascending, ids `<prefix>-<n>`); blank means the explicit `imported_at` argument (the engine never reads a clock).                                                                                                                                      |
| C6  | 05   | Formula guard "exact round trip … names that begin with an apostrophe". | The literal rule cannot round-trip `'=x`. Export adds one apostrophe when the cell matches `^'*[=+\-@\t\r]`; import removes one when it matches `^'+[=+\-@\t\r]`. Identical to the literal rule for every cell without a leading apostrophe, and a bijection overall. Import trims before un-guarding.                                     |
| C7  | 05   | Shortlist columns.                                                      | `score` = score/10 with one decimal (as the UI shows it), blank when unscored; `strongest_evidence` = the computed component with the largest `weight × value` (ties: spec order), worded by `explain.ts`; `missing_information` = every null component with its reason; `relevant_roles` blank when null. Text cells are formula-guarded. |
| C8  | 05   | Export framing.                                                         | CRLF line endings with a trailing CRLF; UTF-8 BOM only on request (`bom: true`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| C9  | 05   | Shortlisted company absent from the analysis.                           | Reported in `unknown_company_ids`, not silently dropped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## C. Where to look first if the Python engine disagrees

Not pinned by the current vectors and most likely to differ: **E1** (key separator), **E2/E3**
(company assignment and aliases under renames and duplicates; no vector renames the company of an
id-keyed role at all, so even "latest name wins" is unexercised), **E4/E5** (absent keys;
`not_same` after merges), **E8** (set vs multiset), **E12** (trim set), **E16** (URL rule),
**E23** (`relevant_roles` 0 vs null with no active role). A vector for each would settle it.
